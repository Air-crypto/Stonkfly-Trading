# Native count differences in the recipient diagnostic

All intervals are relative to the start of one 500 ms observation. Native counts resolve 10 ms bins, not exact spike times or within-bin causal order. Voltages are sampled at bin ends and can miss threshold crossings and resets. Output-neuron count differences are intermediate activity, not new trading decisions; the fixed decoder emits one action after 500 ms. Later observations carry activity from earlier ones. This post-hoc analysis adds no model runs, trading returns or policy selection.
